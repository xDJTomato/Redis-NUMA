Methodology

The benchmark emulates a two-tier NUMA system: a small fast DRAM tier (~50% of the working set) and a large slow CXL tier. All keys cold-start on CXL. Migration strategies promote hot keys into DRAM and (CAAT only) demote cold keys back to CXL to keep DRAM optimally utilized. Net cost = modeled access latency/bandwidth cost + migration cost. Lower net cost is better.
